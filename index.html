Before you start

Finish room setup on the headset, including your walls and furniture. Rifts open on those surfaces and on the floor.

Allow access to spatial data if the browser asks. Without it, the game can't see your room.

Controls

Aim with the laser and pull the trigger to select or shoot. Press grip to pause and open the menu.

How to play

Listen for ghosts and shoot them before they reach you. One touch ends the game. Rifts open and close as you play, and tougher ghosts join over time.
