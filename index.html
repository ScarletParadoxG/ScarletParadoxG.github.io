Ghost Hunter

Ghosts are tearing through your room. Listen for them, and shoot them before they reach you.

Before you start

Finish room setup on your headset, including your walls and furniture. Rifts open on those surfaces and on the floor. Allow access to spatial data if the browser asks.

How to play

Aim with the laser and pull the trigger to shoot. One touch from a ghost ends the game, and tougher ghosts join the longer you survive. There's no pausing, so press the Meta button if you need to quit mid-game.

Know your enemies

  • Ghost: round with two eyes. Takes 2 hits.
  • Wisp: a fast, weaving teardrop with one eye. Takes 1 hit.
  • Phantom: huge, slow and toothy, with a jagged hem. Takes 5 hits.
